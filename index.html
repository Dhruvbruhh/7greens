About

Fifteen years of building solar that runs.

Seven Greens Solar Systems Pvt. Ltd. is a Mumbai-based clean-tech company founded in 2010. We design, engineer, project-manage and maintain solar power assets for residential, commercial, industrial and government clients.

Our ISO 9001:2015 quality system covers every stage — from the first load calculation to commissioning and long-term operations — so a plant we hand over keeps producing for decades, not seasons.

  • In-house design and engineering team
  • Government and institutional project experience
  • Offices in Mumbai and Gandhinagar
  • End-to-end EPC under one accountable roof
